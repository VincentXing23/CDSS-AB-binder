# Fictional clinical interface fixture

These three short questions are original demonstration content, not examination
items or participant data. They illustrate the collection interface using no
advice (T0), correct advice (T1), and deliberately incorrect advice (T2).

Reference answers are A, B, A. The T2 suggestion of thalassaemia trait alone is
intentionally wrong for the constructed example. The very low ferritin supports
iron depletion; the fixture does not claim that coexisting diagnoses can be
excluded in real patients.

Clinical facts used to prepare the example:

- [WHO ferritin guideline](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240000124):
  low ferritin indicates depleted iron stores; 6 ng/mL equals 6 micrograms/L.
- [UCSF Hospital Handbook: Approach to Anemia](https://hospitalhandbook.ucsf.edu/02-approach-anemia/02-approach-anemia):
  an MCV below 80 fL is classified as microcytic.

The exercise demonstrates software behaviour. The three questions are related,
not a counterbalanced experiment, and their response statistics are descriptive.
